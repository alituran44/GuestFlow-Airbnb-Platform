Privacy Policy (GDPR & KVKK)

Last Updated: August 24, 2026 | Compliant with GDPR, CCPA & KVKK

At HostifyOS.com (operated by Ali Turan Inc., Email: hostifyos@gmail.com), protecting the privacy and personal data of our hosts and their guests is a core commitment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Host Information: Name, email address, property details, Wi-Fi credentials (stored encrypted), house rules, and subscription preferences.
  • Guest Information: Check-in timestamps, digital guidebook views, room service requests, and feedback ratings. We do not sell guest data to third parties.
  • Payment Data: All financial transactions are processed by certified Merchant of Record partners (Paddle.com / Lemon Squeezy). HostifyOS does not store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Information

We process information to deliver software features, process payments, provide customer support, optimize performance, and send transactional service updates.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data transmitted between your browser, guest devices, and HostifyOS servers is encrypted using 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol. Sensitive data (Wi-Fi passwords, door access codes) is encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential cookies to maintain secure host portal sessions and lightweight, privacy-focused analytics to monitor system health and speed.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under global data privacy laws, hosts and guests have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of their personal data. To request data deletion, contact us at hostifyos@gmail.com.
